Thank you for your interest in contributing to Envoryx ("the Project"),
maintained by Stefan Mertens ("the Maintainer").

This agreement clarifies the intellectual property rights granted with
contributions from any person or entity. It protects you, the Maintainer and
the Project's users, and it keeps the Maintainer able to steward the Project
long-term – including offering it under additional licenses or transferring it
to a successor – without having to track down every past contributor.

You keep the copyright in your work. You do **not** transfer or assign
anything. You grant the Maintainer a license, and in return the Maintainer
commits to keeping your contribution available as open source (see §5).

The agreement comes in two versions. Sign the **Individual** version if you
contribute on your own behalf. If you contribute as part of your employment or
otherwise on behalf of a company or organisation, that organisation must sign
the **Corporate** version and list you as an authorised contributor.

Contributors sign electronically through the CLA bot on the pull request.
Each pull request from an unsigned contributor is held until the agreement is
signed; you only sign once.
